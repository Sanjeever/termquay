## SSH workspace monitoring

- See CPU, memory, network and disk I/O trends together with root filesystem usage. The overview switches to two columns when the monitoring panel is wide enough.
- Compare all four charts at the same sample time. Switch between 1, 3 and 6 minutes or click to hold a sample. Focus **Live** to step through samples with the arrow keys, and select it to resume.
- Expand a chart to zoom into a time range. The terminal stays beside the expanded chart on wide windows. Narrow windows show the chart over the workspace, and switching between widths preserves the selected sample.
- Keep the collected history visible when sampling fails. Gaps distinguish missing data from measured zero values.

## SSH 工作区监控

- 同时查看 CPU、内存、网络和磁盘 I/O 趋势，以及根文件系统用量。监控面板拖宽后，概览自动切换为双列。
- 四图联动显示同一时刻的样本。可切换 1、3、6 分钟范围，点击锁定样本。聚焦「实时」后可用左右方向键逐点查看，点击恢复跟随。
- 放大单图后可缩放时间范围。宽窗口保留并排终端，窄窗口在工作区内覆盖显示。宽窄切换时保留选中的样本。
- 采集失败时保留已有历史。趋势在缺测处断开，实测零值仍正常显示。
